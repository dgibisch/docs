This takes you from nothing to an app that shows up in everydeck. Your coding agent does the technical work. You click, paste and approve.

## Before you start

- An app you build with an AI coding agent, for example in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Lovable.
- If your app uses Supabase: access to its Supabase project.
- For Lovable: your app must use your own Supabase project. See [Lovable](/connect/lovable).

## Five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.everydeck.ai](https://app.everydeck.ai) and create an account with your email and a password, or continue with Google or GitHub. With email, you get a confirmation link that works for 1 hour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your app">
    The first page says **Let's put your first app on deck**. Type the name of your app and click **Continue**. Nothing is installed yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AI tool">
    everydeck asks **Which AI do you build your app with?** Pick your tool. The page shows the exact way for that tool, for example a command for Claude Code or a button for Cursor.

    Your browser then opens a page that says your tool wants to connect to everydeck. Click **Allow**. Your tool acts as you and only sees apps you can open.

    Each tool has its own page under [Connect your tool](/connect/claude-code).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Supabase (only if your app uses it)">
    If your app runs on Supabase, click **Connect Supabase**, allow it at Supabase and choose the project of your app. everydeck reads only the structure of that project, meaning the names of tables and columns. It never reads data through this connection.

    If your app does not use Supabase, click **My app doesn't use Supabase**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the sentence and approve">
    Copy the sentence from the setup page and paste it into your AI tool. That is the last thing you type.

    Your agent reads your app. When it is done, the setup page says **Your turn: one click**. Look at what everydeck will see and do, then click **Approve**. When you approve, everydeck adds its own parts to your Supabase project, as listed on that page. Nothing of your app is changed or removed.

    After that you can close the page. Your agent goes on by itself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When it is done

The setup page says **Acme is on deck** (with the name of your app). It tells you what is left for you, usually one of these:

- **Merge a pull request.** Your agent opened a pull request on GitHub. Open it and click **Merge**. See [Apps on GitHub and a host](/connect/github-and-hosting).
- **Nothing.** If your repository has no remote, your agent commits the change itself and restarts the app. See [Local apps without GitHub](/connect/local-apps).
- **Add settings at your host.** If your own server needs everydeck settings, the page shows them with **Show the values**.

Click **Open Acme** to see your app. The overview says **Waiting for the first visit to Acme** until the first visit arrives.

<Note>
  If some parts of your app are not connected yet, the end page says so, for example **Acme is on deck, 2 parts are still open**. You can finish them later on the [Setup page](/using/setup-page).
</Note>
