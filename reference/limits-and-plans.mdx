**everydeck is free in this first version.** Free is the only plan. There are no prices, no upgrades and no bills.

> Free: one app per account, 10,000 events a month and 30 days of raw data.

If you need more, write to [support@everydeck.ai](mailto:support@everydeck.ai).

You see your plan and this month's events under **Settings › Team › Billing**. Every limit, with your current usage, is under **All limits** on the same page. Only Owners can open it. A row is marked **from 80 %** and **reached** when the limit is reached.

## What counts as an event

Events are what your app sends to everydeck: errors, signals of your flows, logs and breadcrumbs.

These **never count**: heartbeats, page loads, visits and sign-ins. They also keep arriving when your monthly limit is reached. Rejected events do not count either.

## One app per account

Free includes one app. A second app is refused on every way: in everydeck, through your coding agent and with `npx everydeck init`. The message says: "Free includes one app. Remove the other app, or write to support@everydeck.ai if you need more."

All workspaces where you are Owner count together, so a second workspace is no way around it. Demo apps do not count. A new version of the same app is fine.

The tables below list every limit you, the visitors of your app or your coding agent can reach, on the Free plan.

<Note>
  Many more limits exist for what you or your coding agent can enter at once, for example a reason (300 or 500 characters) or a note on a person (2,000 characters). The full list is under **All limits** on **Settings › Team › Billing**. Above them, the input is rejected and nothing is saved. There is no email, because you see it right away.
</Note>
