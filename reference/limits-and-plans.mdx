**everydeck is free in this first version.** Free is the only plan. There are no prices, no upgrades and no bills.

> Free: one app per account, 10,000 events a month and 30 days of raw data.

If you need more, write to [support@everydeck.ai](mailto:support@everydeck.ai).

You see every limit, with your current usage, under **Settings › Workspace › Plan and limits**. Only Owners can open it. A row turns light yellow from 80 % and light orange when the limit is reached.

## What counts as an event

Events are what your app sends to everydeck: errors, signals of your flows, logs and breadcrumbs.

These **never count**: heartbeats, page loads, visits and sign-ins. They also keep arriving when your monthly limit is reached. Rejected events do not count either.

## One app per account

Free includes one app. A second app is refused on every way: in everydeck, through your coding agent and with `npx everydeck init`. The message says: "Free includes one app. Remove the other app, or write to support@everydeck.ai if you need more."

All workspaces where you are Owner count together, so a second workspace is no way around it. Demo apps do not count. A new version of the same app is fine.

## All limits

The tables below list every limit you, the visitors of your app or your coding agent can reach, on the Free plan.

| Limit | Free | What happens when it is reached | Email |

| Monthly limit reached | 100 % | Logs and breadcrumbs are dropped and appear as a data gap; errors and signals are still accepted. | yes |

| Reachability checks per app | 1 | Another check cannot be created. | no |
| Shortest check interval | 5 min | A reachability check runs at most this often. | no |
| Heartbeat interval | 5 min | A part of your app counts as silent after two missed heartbeats; you see a data gap and a banner. | yes |
| Recorded visits per app and day | 50,000 | Further page views and sign-ins that day are only counted: they are missing in Live and as activity. | yes |
| Measured page loads per app and day | 20,000 | Further page loads that day are only counted, not measured. | yes |
| Different pages in page speed per app and day | 200 | Further pages that day are grouped as "Other pages". | yes |

<Note>
  Many more limits exist for what you or your coding agent can enter at once, for example a reason (300 or 500 characters) or a note on a person (2,000 characters).
