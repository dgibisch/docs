---
title: "Removing everydeck from your app"
description: "Take everything everydeck added out of your app: functions, the outbox, triggers, settings and keys. Your app keeps running as before."
---

You can take everydeck out of your app at any time. Your app keeps running as before. everydeck stops receiving and reading.

Go to **Settings › Acme › General** and find **Remove everydeck from Acme**. Only Owners can do this.

"Takes out everything everydeck added to the app: functions, the outbox, triggers, settings and keys. Your app keeps running as before."

## With a Supabase connection

Click **Remove everydeck**. everydeck removes its parts through the Supabase connection of your project, one by one, and then disconnects. Each part shows **removed**, **was not there** or **not removed** with the reason.

What it removes:

- schedules and triggers,
- the schemas `everydeck_outbox`, `everydeck_door` and `everydeck_read`,
- the role `everydeck_reader`,
- the function `public.everydeck_server_config()`,
- Vault entries, its entries in the migration history,
- its Edge Functions and their settings.

Built-in care:

- Every drop is careful (`RESTRICT`, never `CASCADE`). If something of your app depends on a part, that part stays, and the list says why.
- The extensions `pg_cron` and `pg_net` stay.
- Your scheduled jobs get their own command back first. If a schedule cannot be restored safely, everydeck removes nothing and names the schedule.

When it is done: "everydeck is removed from Acme. Nothing of everydeck is left in the Supabase project."

<Note>
  If everydeck stops in the middle (for example a restart), the card shows "The removal stopped" after at most 2 minutes, with the reason. Start it again: what is already gone shows as **was not there**.
</Note>

## Without a connection

The card says everydeck has no connection to your Supabase project, and shows three steps to do it yourself:

1. In the SQL editor of the project, run the SQL shown.
2. Delete the functions and settings with the Supabase CLI.
3. In your repository, delete the files listed.

Or copy the task for your coding agent. Then click **I removed it myself**.

<Warning>
  Also delete the listed files in your repository, or everydeck comes back with the next migration.
</Warning>

## What happens on everydeck's side

In both cases, everydeck revokes all keys of the app, disconnects the read bridge and app bridges, turns off the change door and disconnects coding agents.

## Disconnecting Supabase only

**Disconnect** under **Settings › App › Connections** deletes everydeck's access keys to Supabase. What everydeck added stays in your project. Remove it with the steps above, like any migration.

To delete the app in everydeck too, see [Deleting your account or app](/reference/delete-account-or-app).
