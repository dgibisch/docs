## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add everydeck">
    On the setup page, choose **Cursor** and click **Add to Cursor**. Confirm the install in Cursor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    When Cursor asks you to sign in, your browser opens. Click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the sentence">
    Paste the sentence from the setup page into Cursor, in the folder of your app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

No model was measured with Cursor, so the setup page gives no model note for it. Whatever model you use, everydeck shows you what is still open after the setup.
