title: "Email delivery of your app"
description: "What everydeck knows about the emails your app sends to its users, what it does not know, and where to see the delivery of everydeck's own emails to your team."
---

There are two kinds of email. They are easy to mix up.

## Emails your app sends

Your app sends its own emails, like a welcome email or a booking confirmation, through a mail provider.

What everydeck knows: if your app map has a flow that sends email, everydeck counts its runs. A run means your app handed the email to the mail provider.

What everydeck does not know:

- whether an email was delivered or bounced,
- whether it landed in spam,
- whether your app sends through Supabase's built-in email service.

everydeck connects to no mail provider. For delivery, look at your mail provider's own dashboard.

<Note>
  Emails that everydeck triggers in your app, like a sign-in link or a password reset, are sent by Supabase Auth as your app's own emails. See [People](/using/people#help-actions).
</Note>

## Emails everydeck sends to your team

Under **Settings › Workspace › Members**, Owners see **Recent deliveries**: every email everydeck sent to your team, with its result:

- "accepted by the mail transport"
- "rejected"
- "outcome unconfirmed"

If an email is rejected, everydeck shows a banner, because another email would not arrive either. It ends with the next email that is delivered.

Which emails everydeck sends is on [Emails and notifications](/using/emails-and-notifications).
