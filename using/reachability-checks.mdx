A reachability check calls a public address of your app from the outside, for example `https://app.acme.com/health`. It tells you when your app cannot be reached at all, even if it sends no errors.

## Create a check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card">
    Go to **Technical**, card **Reachability and heartbeats**, part **Reachability checks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the address">
    Type the address under **Public URL (https)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create check (every 300 s)**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only Owners and Admins can create, pause and remove checks. Every change is in the protocol. Roles that may see code can see the checks.

On Free, each app has **1** check, and a check runs at most every **5 minutes**.

The check also appears under **Settings › App › Connections** as "Uptime check:" with its address.

## States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Reachable** | The last call worked. |
| **Unreachable** | Two calls in a row failed. |
| **Unchecked** | Not called yet. |
| **Paused** | You paused it. |

Use **Pause**, **Enable** and **Remove** on each check.

## What counts as success

- An answer with HTTP status 200 to 399.
- everydeck reads only the status and the time until the headers arrive. It does not read the content of the answer and stores nothing of it.
- A check waits at most its timeout, 5 seconds unless set otherwise. A slow address fails after that time and holds up no other checks.
- everydeck follows no redirects and never calls private networks.

## Outage and recovery

- **Two failures in a row** count as an outage. A case opens, with an email (row **App unreachable** in [Emails and notifications](/using/emails-and-notifications)).
- **One success** closes the case ("Reachable again"), with an email (row **Back up**). This also holds after **Pause** and **Enable**.

The case says itself: "with a single probe location a network problem on everydeck's side cannot be ruled out."

While an account is waiting to be deleted, no checks run.

## What it does not check

- The expiry of your SSL certificate or your domain.
- Required pages like an imprint or a privacy page.
- More than one location.
- The content of the answer.
- Services that do not speak HTTP.
