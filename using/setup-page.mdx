title: "Setup page"
description: "The Setup page shows which parts of your app are connected and working, what still needs a decision, when an update of everydeck is available, and how to finish open parts with your coding agent or end a setup."
---

After your first approval, the **Setup** page shows how far your app is connected. At the top it always says how many parts are not connected yet, until everything works. There is no progress bar.

The side bar shows **Setup · 3 open** until everything is done. The overview shows a quiet row with **Finish setup**, which you can hide until something new is open.

## Three tabs

- **To do** (the default): **Needs your decision**, the parts that are **Not connected yet**, and one line about everything else. The order follows what matters most for you: money and scheduled jobs first, then email and push, then ways through the app, then sign-in, AI and data.
- **All parts:** every part in groups: People and sign-in, Ways through the app, Money, Email and push, AI, Background jobs, Data and code.
- **About this setup:** the technical details of the setup.

## States of a part

Each part has exactly one state:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Working | Connected, and everydeck saw real data of exactly this part. |
| Connected, waiting for data | Connected, not used yet. This is normal. |
| Connected, nothing arrives | Your app sent at least 50 signals since the approval, but none from this part for 48 hours. It may be connected in the wrong place. |
| Not connected | Not set up yet. |
| In progress | Your coding agent is working on it. |
| Check: does Acme have this? | A weak finding. You decide. |
| Your decision | Your agent and everydeck disagree. You decide. |
| Not in this app | With the reason and who said it. |
| Not checked yet | everydeck could not check this area. It does not count as missing. |
| Last run failed | A scheduled job reported a failed run. It counts as working again after a run that succeeds. |
| everydeck can't watch this job | Your agent says this job cannot send a heartbeat. |
| Stops | Reading one kind of data from your app stopped 2 runs in a row, with a timeout, an error, or because a single entry is larger than everydeck takes. Everything else is read. In production it also shows on [Needs you](/using/needs-you) and you get an email right away. |

"Connected" is never the goal. "Working" is.

## Finishing open parts

Open a row to see what the part brings, what everydeck found (the place in your code) and what your agent said. Then:

- **Copy prompt for Claude Code** (the button names your connected tool, or says **Copy prompt for your coding agent**). Paste the prompt into your agent in your Acme project.
- **Acme doesn't have this**, with a reason, like "We don't take money yet" or **Something else** with a few words (up to 200 characters). You can undo it.

**Copy prompt for all** sends up to 50 open parts at once, in the order of To do.

The row then updates by itself: "Waiting for Claude Code to pick this up.", "Claude Code is working on it.", "Claude Code is done. everydeck sees more now, so it needs your approval." (with **Review and approve**), **Connected, waiting for data**, **Working**.

- If your agent does not pick it up for 1 day, the row says so and offers the prompt again.
- If your agent goes quiet for 30 minutes after picking it up, the row says it "stopped before it finished" and offers the prompt again: "It picks up where it stopped."

### When your agent and everydeck disagree

If your agent says a part does not apply, but everydeck found it in your code, **Needs your decision** shows both sides. You decide: "Acme has this, copy prompt for Claude Code" or "Claude Code is right".

### Parts only you can do

Some parts are steps at your host, like **Upload key at Vercel**. They show where to add the key, the name to copy and **Show the key**. If your host can sign in by itself and only one switch is off, the part is called **Source maps at Vercel** and shows that switch. You cannot send these to your agent. See [Keys at your host](/connect/github-and-hosting#keys-at-your-host).

Scheduled jobs need no step from you. Your agent adds their heartbeat without a key or variable at your host. See [App parts and heartbeats](/using/app-parts-and-heartbeats#how-scheduled-jobs-report).

## First value

The part **First value** is the flow whose first result is what a new person came for, for example a first CV analyzed. It "Shows on Growth how many new people reach what they came for, and how long it takes. You can also set it under Settings › General." It is done once your agent named it or you chose it there. See [First value](/using/flows-and-journeys#first-value).

## Flows that don't report yet

Every flow people use should be countable on Growth. The part **N flows don’t report yet** lists the flows that send nothing to everydeck and that everydeck cannot see by itself: "Acme doesn’t send these flows to everydeck yet, so Growth can’t count how often people use them. Your coding agent adds a signal to each, or names the AI call that does the work."

A flow is done as soon as it reports, its AI calls name it, or everydeck sees the records it creates. If nobody really does a flow, your agent says so with the place in the code. See [Growth](/using/flows-and-journeys#growth).

## Money not connected

If a money part is open, Money is dimmed in the side bar and the page says "Payments aren't connected yet". If you say your app takes no money, Money disappears from the side bar.

## Update available

When a newer version of everydeck's parts is available for your app, the Setup page shows **Update available**, with what it brings. If it closes a security gap, it says **Security update available**.

You also see it in two other places, once per version:

- **Notifications:** a line "Acme: everydeck 0.5.16 is available" in the bell. It is the only line that never comes as an email. See [Emails and notifications](/using/emails-and-notifications).
- **Overview:** a band "everydeck 0.5.16 is available for Acme." with **See update**. The **x** ("Hide until the next version") hides it for you until the next version. An app without an update shows nothing.

An update can also bring a newer app map. Apps set up before these words existed are offered: "Timelines say what people did (“Analyzed CV”) instead of the name of the flow." and "Growth shows how many new people reach the first value of your app, and how long it takes." You confirm the first value again, like the first time.

An update also offers: "Growth counts every flow people use, also those that send nothing to everydeck today." See [Flows that don't report yet](#flows-that-dont-report-yet).

Click **Update**, then paste the sentence into your coding agent. "Your coding agent builds it as a pull request, the same way as the setup. Nothing changes in Acme until you merge it; then everydeck checks every part again."

### How an update runs

1. **Waiting:** the page says "Update 0.5.15 → 0.5.16" and "Waiting for Claude Code to start the update", with the sentence: "Paste this into Claude Code. This page moves on by itself when it starts." There is nothing to approve yet.
2. **Your agent builds the update:** "Your coding agent reads Acme again and builds the update. everydeck tells it what is new since the last setup."
3. **Your OK, only if everydeck would read more:** if everydeck reads the same as before, the page says "Same access as before" and "everydeck reads nothing new, so your OK from 16:44 still applies." If it would read more, the approval page says "Update 0.5.15 → 0.5.16 for Acme". On top, **What changes since your last OK** lists only what is new, for example "everydeck would also read …", "New flows: …" or "First value: …". Everything else is folded under "Everything else, as you approved it before". The address of the approval ends in `/approve`.
4. **You merge one pull request:** "Nothing changes in Acme until you merge." In an update, everydeck adds its parts to your Supabase project only after your merge. The page says so: "everydeck adds its parts to the Supabase project … after your merge". The single steps (database changes, functions, settings, read bridge and app bridge) stand indented under that line.
5. **Almost live:** once the pull request is merged, the page stays on **Almost live** until every part of your app reports the new version.

Only Owners and Admins see the **Update** button. Updates never come by email. This card is the one place for them.

## End this setup

While a setup is running, the page where you connect your app (the one from the [Quickstart](/quickstart)) has the link **End this setup**. It asks: your agent "stops here and everydeck ends this setup. What is already set up in Acme stays. You can set it up again anytime." Click **End setup**, or **Keep going**.

## Keep everydeck working after every update

Setup also shows the state of the check on every push: **Check on every push**, that it was added and waits for the first push, or when all everydeck calls were in place on the last push. If you said no to the check, the card **Keep everydeck working after every update** offers **Copy the prompt for your coding agent**. See [Apps on GitHub and a host](/connect/github-and-hosting#keep-everydeck-working-after-every-update).

## Reminder email

48 hours after your first approval, if a part is still open, you get one email **Setup isn't finished**. It lists only parts you have not seen here, decided or hidden. By default it comes in your daily summary. You can change or turn it off under **Settings › Notifications**.
