The overview is the first page of each app. It answers: how is my app doing, and does anything need me?

Before the first visit arrives, it says **Waiting for the first visit to Acme**. If no visit arrives for 3 days (since the setup or since the last visit), the overview shows a clear warning with **Check in Setup**.

## Your apps

After you sign in you land on **Your apps**. Each app has a card with its logo, its environment, **Live** with events per minute (or **Quiet** with the last event), a 28-day chart, up to three figures and what needs you. If nothing does, the card says **All good, nothing needs you**.

Sort by **needs you first** or **last activity**.

## At most one band on top

When something is broken, one band sits at the top of the page, never several. It is the first thing everydeck stopped doing for your app (for example "everydeck has not been able to read data from Acme since 4 Nov"), or else the warning that no visits arrive. Each band has one link to where you can act. The band of an unfinished setup shows only when nothing is broken. With up to three open parts it names them, for example "1 part of Acme isn’t connected yet: First value. Until it’s set, Growth can’t show how many new people reach it." It says that pages stay empty only when an open part would bring data. When an update of everydeck is available and nothing else is shown, the band says "everydeck 0.5.16 is available for Acme." with **See update** and an **x** that hides it until the next version.

## The sentence at the top

A short sentence sums up your app. everydeck builds it without AI, from a fixed list of statements. Each one appears only when it is true, for example:

- your app "has been in everydeck for 3 days" (only in its first 7 days),
- "best week yet", or "keeps growing",
- new people compared with the period before,
- **One thing needs you** whenever a case is open,
- new records in your app's data as their own sentence, for example "New in Acme’s data: 1 job." A record read again later, for example after reading works again, is not counted as new.

When your app reports what people did, the sentence tells the week from these facts instead, and each number is a link. For example: "This week 2 organizations worked in Acme: analyzed CV 7× for $0.32. Most active: L1VE GmbH. Nothing needs you." Amounts appear only if you may see money. If your app has sides, like owners and guests, the sentence names each side, for example "This week 1 new owner published a property 1×. 4 new guests sent a message 4×." With no one active it says "No owners or guests were active this week." It never shows a 0.

The words come from your app map: what a person did in each flow, like "Analyzed CV". If your app map does not have these words yet, the name of the flow shows instead. **Update available** on the Setup page offers them. See [Setup page](/using/setup-page#update-available).

## Key numbers

Under the sentence, a row of numbers: new sign-ups (or new people), active people, monthly revenue or revenue, and how many things need you.

If your app has sides, each side gets its own row: **New**, **Active** and how many reached its first value, for example **Owners** and **Guests**. See [First value](/using/flows-and-journeys#first-value).

A number that is not there yet shows as a short grey word, never as a 0. For example **No visits yet**, or for revenue **Not connected**, **No amounts** or **No payment yet**, with a link like **Connect Stripe** (or **Connect payments**) to the setup.

## Periods and comparisons

Choose **Today**, **7 days** or **30 days**.

- **7 and 30 days** compare with the same length of time right before.
- **Today** compares with yesterday from midnight to the same time of day, in your browser's time zone. At 08:00, that is yesterday 00:00 to 08:00. The page says "vs this time yesterday".

A comparison appears only when the earlier period is fully in the data. For 7 days you need 14 days of data. Until then, the legend says when the comparison starts.

## The big chart

One tab for each figure your app has, at most four: active people, new people, monthly revenue (only with plans that have an amount), AI costs (only with recorded AI calls), or the most used flow. Markers show each new version of your app and each case that opened.

With little data, the chart shows a sentence instead of a large area. Before anything is counted it says "Nothing counted in this period yet. The line starts with the first count." With fewer than 3 measured days (or hours, for **Today**) it says what was measured so far, for example "1 person was active since Saturday. The chart starts after a few days." everydeck never draws zeros for "not measured".

## Cards

Each card appears only when it has data. Otherwise the next one moves up.

- **What happened:** day by day, what people did, in your app's words, for example "Annette Hansen · L1VE GmbH opened job “System Integration Engineer” and analyzed CV 4× · $0.18". It shows runs of your flows, AI calls, and records people created in the kinds of data you see in the side bar of your app. Data your app writes in the background, like notifications or technical tables, never shows here. An AI call says what was done ("analyzed CV"), or "used AI for CV scoring" if your app map has no words for it yet, or "used AI" without a flow. A record without a title shows as "created job without a name"; its short address shows only in the developer view. A person without an approved name shows as "Someone". The card shows the last 7 days with events, at most 4 people a day ("1 more person") and 6 lines per person ("and 1 more thing"). With many events it says "Only the latest events of this period are shown." **Open Live** shows everything. Before anything happened it says "Nothing happened in Acme in this period yet. Each thing a person does shows up here in Acme’s words."
- **Needs you:** the open case, or a green area with what was checked and the last case that was repaired. See [Needs you](/using/needs-you).
- **Money and AI costs:** only with payments everydeck saw or plans with an amount. A margin appears only when every AI call of the last 30 days has a known price and revenue is in USD. Under the AI costs, **Who caused them** names the organizations (or, without organizations, the people) with the highest AI costs, with their calls and cost. At most 5 rows, the rest as one line "N more". Calls without an organization or person show as one line. **All calls** opens the list of every AI call. See [Money and AI costs](/using/money-and-ai-costs).
- **Coming back:** how many people came back, once a full week is measured.
- **The way through your main flow:** the first funnel from [Growth](/using/flows-and-journeys).
- **Your newest people**, with their way through the app.
- **In your database:** the kinds of data in your approved read scope.
- **How fast your app is:** duration per run for the three flows with the most measured runs. Under 2 s is "quick", over 4 s is "slow".
- **Where people use Acme:** only if your app runs on more than one platform, like web and iPhone.
- **Happening now:** the latest entries from [Live](/using/live). A line about a person or an organization opens their page. Errors, cases, logs, data gaps and **Open live view** open Live.
- **Most active** organizations, if your app has organizations. Otherwise **What people use most**.

## Footer

The footer shows how many programs of your app are alive, the last sign of life, and the current version with your coding agent and its pull request. **Details in Developer settings** opens the details of each part.

## If you have no access to money

People with the Support role see no revenue, MRR, margin or AI costs on the overview. In their place there is nothing, not a 0. See [Team and roles](/using/team-and-roles).
