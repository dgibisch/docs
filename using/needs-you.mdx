**Needs you** is your to-do list in everydeck. It is on the overview of each app and has its own page. The **Your apps** page counts it across all apps.

## What appears here

- **Cases that need action.** Errors, outages, missed expectations, data loss, security errors and more. See [Cases and repairs](/using/cases-and-repairs).
- **Security:** only points of level Error from Supabase's Security Advisor, together as one case. See [Security](/using/security).
- **New permissions for the change door:** when everydeck can do more than you allowed, it asks here with **Allow** and **Not now**. See [Changing data](/using/changing-data#new-permissions).
- **Reading your data stopped:** "everydeck isn't reading Acme's data", with the reason and the way to fix it.
- **Reading a kind of data doesn't finish:** "everydeck can’t finish reading applications" (in production, after reading it stopped 2 runs in a row). "Everything else from Acme is read; applications in everydeck show their last state. everydeck keeps trying. The setup says why and what helps." The line opens the Setup page, and you get an email right away.
- **A kind of data can't be read:** your database lacks a table or a column that the app map names. everydeck keeps what it read before, reads everything else, and says what to ask your coding agent.
- **Supabase no longer accepts everydeck:** "everydeck can no longer reach the Supabase project of Acme", with **Reconnect**.

## When an error becomes a task

A new error is first **Open · watched**. It shows only on the Errors page, with no task and no email. The case page says: "everydeck is watching this: it touches no flow with business consequences and few people so far. It becomes a task on Needs you when it affects 5 people or grows."

It becomes a task on Needs you, with an email, as soon as one of these is true:

- it hits a flow with business consequences (for example a failed request in a flow that has a result step),
- it affects 5 people,
- it has 10 events in 24 hours.

Once a task, it stays a task. All other kinds of cases become a task right away.

## Order

Needs you is sorted by severity, then by the latest event.

| Severity | Kinds |
|---|---|
| Critical | Errors, outages, moderation, data loss, security |
| Warning | Missed expectations, monitoring gaps, due dates, implausible values |
| Note | Data gaps |

## Clearing it

On each case you can **Mark as fixed**, **Ignore**, or copy a fix for your coding agent. Each action shows a short message with **Undo**. See [Cases and repairs](/using/cases-and-repairs#what-you-can-do).

When nothing needs you, the card turns into a green area with what everydeck checked and the last case it repaired. The page then says **Nothing needs you** and lists **What everydeck watches**: errors, security, your app's programs, the ways through the app, payments and background jobs, each with its latest state.
