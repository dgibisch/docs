If your app groups people into organizations, like teams, companies or workspaces, everydeck shows them under **Organizations**. Everyone who can see the app can open them.

## Where organizations come from

Each organization comes from your app's server events. everydeck knows an organization by the id your server sends with an event.

A person belongs to an organization in two ways, and the page says which:

- **observed:** a server event named both the person and the organization,
- **according to the app:** your app's user data says so.

## Names

The name of an organization comes only from your server: the SDK sends it when your app identifies a person with the name of their organization. Without it, the page says "no name approved", or the organization shows with its short address.

Each organization has a short address like `ORG-3`. It keeps your app's own id out of the address bar and the browser history.

## The page of an organization

- **People:** who belongs to it, with the evidence.
- **Functions used** over the chosen number of days.
- **Problems:** open cases that hit this organization.
- **All runs.**

On the overview, a card shows organizations if your app has them. Otherwise that place shows "What people use most".

## Money per organization

If your app has plans or payments per organization, Owners, Admins, Agency and Viewers see the plan and the revenue. Support sees no amounts. See [Money and AI costs](/using/money-and-ai-costs).

## Deleting an organization's data

Owners and Admins can delete everything everydeck stored about a customer. It works like the [privacy deletion of a person](/using/people#privacy-deletion-of-a-person).

## What there is not

- No name rule for organizations, and no check whether their names arrive.
- The read bridge delivers no organization names for this page.
- everydeck does not merge two ids of the same organization.
