title: "Weekly email"
description: "Every Monday at 8:00 in your time zone, everydeck sends one email per app with new people, runs that worked, money, what is open and what was fixed."
---

Every Monday at 8:00 in your time zone, everydeck sends one email per app. It sums up the week before and compares it with the week before that.

## What is in it

- The subject: the app and the headline of the week, like "Acme this week: …". If something is open for you, it says how many things are for you.
- **New people**, compared with last week.
- The main action of your app, like "Checkout: 40 this week".
- **Runs that worked**.
- Money, only if your app measures it: **People who paid** and **Monthly revenue now**.
- What is open, with since when, and what your coding agent waits for.
- "Fixed this week": cases that were fixed.
- New cases, or "No new case opened this week."

The footer says "Every Monday at 8:00 your time", with your time zone.

## The week and the time

- The week runs from Monday to Sunday in the time zone of your profile. Two people in different time zones each get their own week.
- The email goes out on Monday between 8:00 and 9:00 your time. If it could not be sent in that hour, the week is skipped. A missed week is not sent later.
- The first email comes after the first full week since your app went live. There is none during the setup.
- Your time zone is taken from your browser the first time you open everydeck. You can change it in your profile. Without a time zone, the email comes at 8:00 UTC.

## Who gets it

Owners and Admins of apps they can see. Only apps with an approved app map get a weekly email.

Someone new in the team gets their first weekly email about a week in which they were already a member.

## Turning it off

Turn off the row **Weekly report** under **Settings › Notifications**, for all apps or one app. Or use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of the email. See [Emails and notifications](/using/emails-and-notifications#unsubscribe).

The weekly report has no "right away" or "daily summary" choice. It is on or off.

## What there is not

- The weekly email is not in the bell. Each thing in it is there already on its own.
- No other channel than email.
