title: "Team and roles"
description: "Invite your team to everydeck with one of five roles: Owner, Admin, Support, Agency or Viewer. Limit access per app, see connected coding agents and work in several workspaces."
---

Invite people under **Settings**, in the group of your team, **Members**. **Members** is always there, also while you are alone: "Only you so far. Invite someone to open your apps with you; you choose their role and which apps they see."

## The five roles

| Role | What it may do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including plan and limits, deleting apps and the account. |
| **Admin** | Everything in the apps they can see: approve changes, edit, suspend, delete. Not plan and limits. |
| **Support** | People, chats and listings: answer, suspend, send sign-in links, remove messages. Sees no money and no code. |
| **Agency** | Errors, flows and the connected coding agent for the client's app. Can fix, can't delete people. |
| **Viewer** | Sees everything in the apps they can see, changes nothing. |

A Viewer also sees money and code. Owners always see all apps.

Some actions are only for Owners: inviting people and changing roles, creating apps, connecting GitHub, **Billing**, exporting and deleting the account, deleting an app.

## Inviting someone

Only Owners can invite. Click **Invite**, enter the address, pick a role and choose **All apps of this workspace** or **Only these apps**. Then click **Send invitation**.

"The invitation goes to exactly this address and can only be accepted by an everydeck account that confirmed it. It is valid for 7 days."

- The link is shown once, so you can pass it on yourself. The link alone grants nothing: only an account with the confirmed address can accept.
- There is at most one open invitation per address. **Resend** sends a new one, and the old link stops working. **Remove** withdraws it: "The link in the email stops working."
- If an invitation expires, the person who sent it gets an email.

### When someone accepts

Both sides get an email right away:

- the new member: their role, the apps they can see, and that emails start now, with nothing from before they joined,
- the person who invited them: "… accepted your invitation to Acme", with a button **Open Members**.

The new member's first daily summary comes at the next 8:00 in their time zone. See [Emails and notifications](/using/emails-and-notifications#new-members).

## Changing a role or apps

Use the menu next to each person. Every change appears in **Changes to the team**, with the value before and after. Only Owners see this list.

When a role goes down, or an app is taken away, everydeck revokes the coding agent keys this person may no longer have.

**The last owner stays owner.** Make someone else owner first, then change your own role.

## Removing someone, or leaving

- **Remove** (Owners only): "Access ends with the next request, also for open sessions and coding agents connected by this person. Coding agent keys this person issued are revoked; the keys of your apps stay."
- **Leave**: any member can leave. To come back you need a new invitation.

## Connected coding agents

The page shows **Coding agents connected to your apps**. Click **Manage and disconnect** to see each agent with the person, the apps and when it was last seen.

Only the person who connected an agent can **Disconnect** it. As an Owner, you end someone else's agent by removing the person, taking the app away from them, or removing everydeck from the app.

## Several workspaces

You can be a member of several workspaces, for example Owner in your own and Admin in a client's. Each workspace has its own address. Opening it shows exactly this workspace under **Your apps**. From two workspaces on, a switch at the top left changes between them.

Every email names the workspace it was sent for: "Sent for the workspace Acme in everydeck."

## Second factor

Set up two-factor authentication under **Settings › Sign-in and security**, with an authenticator app. When it is on: "everydeck asks for a code from your authenticator app when you sign in, when you connect a coding agent, and before approvals that give it new rights in your apps."

- **Add another device**, so you are not locked out if you lose one.
- **Recovery codes**: each code turns two-factor authentication off once, if you lose your phone.
- You get an email each time your second factor is turned on, off, or a device is added or removed.

A confirmation stays fresh for 10 minutes. Without a second factor you can still approve, and the approval is marked "approved without a second factor".

## Signing in to everydeck

Sign in with email and password, or **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**. There is no magic link, no Apple, no single sign-on and no SMS for everydeck accounts.

To change your password, go to **Settings › Sign-in and security** and click **Send link**. After setting a new one, all other sessions are signed out.

The same page shows **Where you are signed in**. **Sign out everywhere else** ends all other sessions. A sign-in from a new browser or system gets you an email.

### Signing out and switching accounts

Open the menu behind your profile picture and click **Sign out**, or **Switch account**. Signing out clears everything everydeck kept in this browser for your account. If you then sign in with another account, you land on **Your apps** of that account.

If an approval in your coding agent went to the wrong account, use **Not you?** and **Switch account** on the approval page.

## What it does not do

- No custom roles or single permissions.
- Admins cannot invite.
- No direct disconnect of someone else's agent.
- No SSO or SCIM for teams.
