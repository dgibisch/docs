## Flows

"A flow is something your app does again and again; a run is one time it happened." For example signing in, creating a listing or paying.

Flows come from your approved app map. A flow without a signal shows as "Planned, not yet instrumented". Sign-in, sign-up and the core object of your app (what people create most) are always built in, never only planned.

### How a run ends

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Result in time | The result arrived within its deadline. |
| Result recorded | The server reported the result. There is no deadline to judge. |
| Result late | The result came after its deadline. |
| Failed | A declared failure, or the deadline passed without a result. |
| Ended without result | Another end of your app, neither result nor failure. |
| In progress, then Completion not confirmed | No end and no new evidence for 30 minutes. Neither success nor failure. |
| Hung | A request of this run got no answer for at least 30 seconds. |
| Not judgeable | A contradiction or a data gap over the deadline. |

A result reported only by the browser is marked "browser only".

### The Flows page

Choose 7 or 30 days. The page starts with one sentence, like "3 of 5 flows ran in the last 30 days; 2 runs failed." Below stand **Runs**, **Failed**, **Succeeded** and **Open cases**.

- **Ran in the last 30 days**: each flow with runs, failed, succeeded, median and p95 duration, runs per day and the state.
- **No runs in the last 30 days**: each flow with its known steps and how many have a signal. A flow without a signal says "Planned, not yet instrumented".
- **Runs without a flow**: runs everydeck could not assign to a flow of the app map, with **View in runs**.

Durations come from your app's own clock. If your app runs on more than one platform, you can filter by platform.

### Requests without an answer

If a request in a flow waits 30 seconds without an answer, the run ends as **Hung**. everydeck never cancels or changes your app's request. One hang is no alarm. From 3 runs with a hang at the same place in 24 hours, a case opens with an email. "No error was thrown, so error trackers stay silent."

### Silent jobs

Background jobs of your app (cron, scheduled functions, workers) send a heartbeat. If a job misses two of its runs, a case opens, for example "Expire trials has not reported since Sunday 1 Nov." It closes by itself when the job reports again. This runs in production only. Each schedule, its runs and its case are on [Schedules](/using/schedules).

## Growth

**Growth** shows where new people come from, whether they come back, and where people get lost in the main flows of your app. It is in the side bar under App.

Choose the last 7, 30 (the default) or 90 days. If your app runs on more than one platform, you can filter by platform.

- **The sentence at the top,** for example "In the last 30 days 120 people used Acme. 40 were new, most of them from Google." and where most get lost.
- **Four numbers:** **New people**, **Active people**, **Came back within 7 days** and **Became paying** (only if your app measures money), each compared with the days before.
- **New people by source:** new people per day by where they came from, with a mark when a campaign started.
- **Where they come from:** the first visit of each new person, by channel (like Search, Social, AI assistants, Referral, Email or Direct) and source, from the domain of the referring page and the UTM values, never its path or query. This works by itself. To tell two of your own posts apart, add `?utm_campaign=launch` to the link.
- **Campaigns** (from `utm_campaign`) and **First page they saw**, once there are any.
- **Do they come back?** For the new people of each week, the share that was active again in each following week. It appears once enough new people have arrived.
- **Most used pages,** with visits, people and **Loads in**: the time until the main content shows, for three of four visits. A dash means too few measured visits so far. Click a page to see its load times in detail.
- **Funnels:** chains of flows from your app map, "the same people, step by step". The step that loses the most people is marked. **See the n** opens the list of these people.
- **Identity check,** if your app has one: how many active people are verified, and why the others stopped.

With little data, a card shows one sentence instead of an empty chart.

## Active and new

"Active" means at least one visit in the period (30 days by default), or anything else the person did in your app. "New" means signed up in that period. The full definitions are on [Numbers and growth](/using/numbers#what-active-and-new-mean).

Page loads, visits and sign-ins never count toward your monthly events. Page speed in detail is on [Source maps and page speed](/developers/source-maps-and-web-vitals#page-speed).

## What it does not do

- No custom funnels or custom alerts. everydeck knows deadlines between two signals, silent signals after a new version and silent jobs.
- No split by language or country, only by platform.
- No session recordings, rage clicks or form drop-offs.
- People without an id count in no funnel.
