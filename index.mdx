You built an app with an AI coding agent, like Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Lovable. Now people use it. You want to know what they do, what breaks, and how to fix it, without building your own admin panel.

That is what everydeck is for.

## What you get

everydeck sets up the back office of your app for you. After the setup you open [app.everydeck.ai](https://app.everydeck.ai) and see:

- **What people do, live.** Visits, sign-ins, the main actions of your app, as they happen. See [Live](/using/live).
- **What breaks, as cases.** Errors are grouped into cases, with who was hit and the way there. One click copies a fix for your coding agent. See [Cases and repairs](/using/cases-and-repairs).
- **Who uses your app.** A page for each person, with what they did and what went wrong for them. See [People](/using/people).
- **Your app's own data.** The records of your app, like orders, listings or bookings, read only. See [Your app's data](/using/your-data).
- **Changes you approve.** Your team can edit records, send a sign-in link or suspend someone, but only what you switched on. See [Changing data](/using/changing-data).
- **Security advice.** What Supabase's Security Advisor reports about your database, in plain words. See [Security](/using/security).
- **Emails when it matters.** A bundled email for new cases, an email when a fix worked, and an email when everydeck stops something for your app. See [Emails and notifications](/using/emails-and-notifications).
- **Your limits, in the open.** Every limit you can reach, with its value and what happens. See [Limits and plans](/reference/limits-and-plans).

## How it gets into your app

You do not install everydeck yourself. Your coding agent does it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your coding agent">
    You add everydeck to your AI tool once. Your tool talks to everydeck over MCP, a standard way for AI tools to use other services.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste one sentence">
    You paste one sentence into your AI tool. Your agent reads your app and writes down what it found.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve once">
    You look at what everydeck will see and do, and click **Approve**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your agent does the rest">
    Your agent adds everydeck to your code and opens a pull request. You merge it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart).

## What everydeck does not do

- everydeck never writes code in your app by itself. Your coding agent writes the code, and you merge it.
- everydeck never creates an account in your app and never signs in there.
- everydeck never stores messages, passwords, card numbers or the values from the `.env` files of your app.
- everydeck never deploys your app. You stay in charge of what goes live.
